# Privacy Policy for Isle / Isle 隐私政策

**Last updated / 最后更新：October 14, 2026 / 2026 年 10 月 14 日**

---

## English

Isle ("the app") does not collect, store, or share your personal data. The developer has no access to your data.

### What stays on your device

- Timer settings, focus session records, task labels, achievements and statistics are stored on your device (SwiftData and UserDefaults).
- The app shares the current timer state with its widget through an App Group container (`group.com.guoxj.isle`). This data does not leave your device.
- Notification and sound preferences are stored locally.

Apart from Apple-provided services listed below (iCloud, WeatherKit, Health, Calendar), the app does not communicate with any server. There is no developer-operated backend.

### iCloud sync (optional, on by default)

- When "iCloud Sync" is on, your settings and session records sync across your own devices through Apple's iCloud (CloudKit and iCloud key-value storage). The data is stored in your private iCloud account and the developer cannot read it.
- Turn it off any time in **Settings → Privacy → iCloud Sync**. To remove already-synced data, use iOS **Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage**.

### Location and weather (optional)

- If you allow location access, the app requests **kilometer-level accuracy** and sends that location to Apple's WeatherKit to show local weather in the island scene. The location is not stored and is not used for anything else.
- Turn weather off in **Settings → Privacy**, or deny location access in iOS Settings. The timer works fully without it.

### Apple Health (optional)

- With "Health Writing" on and your permission, the app writes your focus sessions to Apple Health as **Mindful Minutes**. It does **not** read your health data.
- Turn it off in **Settings → Privacy → Health Writing**, or revoke access in iOS **Settings → Health**.

### Calendar (optional, off by default)

- When enabled, the app writes your focus sessions to a calendar on your device.

### Co-Focus (optional)

- Co-Focus lets you focus together with friends nearby. It uses Apple's local networking (Bluetooth / local Wi-Fi) to discover and connect to participants.
- While Co-Focus is active, the display name you choose and your current focus status are shared **only** with participants on the same local network. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any server.

### What the app never does

- No account or sign-in.
- No advertising, no analytics, and no tracking or third-party SDKs.
- No selling or sharing of your data with the developer or third parties.

### Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

### Changes

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

### Contact

Questions or requests? Please open an issue at <https://github.com/guoxiujiang1971/isle/issues>

## 中文

Isle（下称「本 App」）不收集、不存储、不共享你的个人数据，开发者无法访问你的数据。

### 只留在你设备上的内容

- 计时设置、专注记录、任务标签、成就与统计数据保存在你的设备上（SwiftData 与 UserDefaults）。
- 本 App 通过 App 群组容器（`group.com.guoxj.isle`）与它的小组件共享当前计时状态，该数据不离开你的设备。
- 通知与声音偏好保存在本地。

除下列 Apple 提供的服务（iCloud、WeatherKit、健康、日历）外，本 App 不与任何服务器通信，也没有任何开发者自建的后端。

### iCloud 同步（可选，默认开启）

- 开启「iCloud 同步」时，你的设置与专注记录会通过 Apple iCloud（CloudKit 与 iCloud 键值存储）在**你自己的设备之间**同步。数据存放在你的私有 iCloud 账户中，开发者无法读取。
- 可在 **设置 → 隐私 → iCloud 同步** 随时关闭。要删除已同步的数据，请使用 iOS **设置 → 你的名字 → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间**。

### 定位与天气（可选）

- 若你允许定位，本 App 只请求**公里级精度**的位置，并把它发送给 Apple 的 WeatherKit，用于在岛屿场景中显示当地天气。位置不会被存储，也不用于其他用途。
- 可在 **设置 → 隐私** 中关闭天气，或在 iOS 设置中拒绝定位。拒绝后计时功能完全不受影响。

### Apple 健康（可选）

- 开启「健康写入」并授权后，本 App 会把专注记录以「**正念分钟**」写入 Apple 健康，**不会读取**你的健康数据。
- 可在 **设置 → 隐私 → 健康写入** 中关闭，或在 iOS **设置 → 健康** 中撤销授权。

### 日历（可选，默认关闭）

- 开启后，本 App 会把专注记录写入你设备上的日历。

### 一起专注 Co-Focus（可选）

- Co-Focus 让你与附近的朋友一起专注，使用 Apple 的本地网络能力（蓝牙 / 局域网）发现并连接参与者。
- Co-Focus 开启期间，**只有**同一局域网内的参与者能看到你设置的昵称与当前专注状态。不会把任何数据发送给开发者或服务器。

### 本 App 绝不做的事

- 没有账号、没有登录。
- 没有广告、没有分析统计、没有追踪或第三方 SDK。
- 不会把你的数据出售或提供给开发者或第三方。

### 儿童

本 App 不收集任何人的信息（包括儿童），也不会有意这样做。

### 变更

若本政策变更，会在本页面更新并修改「最后更新」日期。

### 联系方式

有任何问题或请求，请在 <https://github.com/guoxiujiang1971/isle/issues> 提交 issue。
