# Privacy Policy for Isle

**Last updated: June 21, 2026**

Isle does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

## Data Storage

- All timer configurations and session statistics are stored locally on your device using SwiftData and UserDefaults.
- No data is transmitted to external servers.
- No analytics or tracking SDKs are used.

## App Group

Isle uses an App Group container (`group.com.guoxj.isle`) to share timer state between the main app and its widget extension. This data never leaves your device.

## Third-Party Services

Isle does not integrate any third-party services that would collect user data.

## Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, updates will be reflected on this page.

## Contact

If you have questions, please open an issue on the GitHub repository.
